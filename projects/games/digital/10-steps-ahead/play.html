10 Steps Ahead

An arcade puzzle game with global highscores



Overview


(the APK works better; download below)

An Android-port-in-progress of an arcade puzzle game I made in 2020.The aim is to survive for as many steps as possible. If even one attack from the totem hits you through the ring of barriers, you are af!

Details about the different attacks and powerups can be found by pressing the in-game ℹ️ button.


Every 24 hours, the game gets a new daily seed and daily leaderboard, which can be viewed in-game. Additionally, there is an all-time leaderboard that is shown in-game *and* on the sidebar to the left of this text!

Patch Notes


12/06/2026

Content changes:

  • The Hammer+ perk now causes Hammers to revive barriers to 2 HP, instead of 1.
  • You can now pick your own seed, or even copy the seed of any highscore entry!

QoL improvements:

  • To prevent accidents, the perk window now becomes clickable after a 1 second delay.

Bugfixes:

  • Portals will no longer spawn behind fallen barriers.


11/06/2026

Content changes:

  • New totem layer: COMBINER - Triggers the two layers above it simultaneously.
  • New totem layer: HORIZON BEAM - Deals 1 damage on opposite sides of the field.
  • New perk: MAGNET+ - While warping with a Magnet, restore +1 HP to ALL barriers you pass.

Visual clarity improvements:

  • More consistent totem layer colouring: red now means "this can directly kill you".

Bugfixes:

  • Portals will no longer spawn behind fallen barriers.


10/06/2026

Content changes:

  • You can now play with random seeds! (though your score won't count for the daily leaderboard)

Balancing changes:

  • The Bridge powerup now restores +3 HP to all affected barriers, but appears less often.
  • Made the totem's starting sequence a bit more satisfying to work with.

Visual clarity improvements:

  • New visual effects for when a barrier takes damage or is repaired!
  • Powerup slots are now visible while empty, improving the Extra Powerup perk experience.
  • The Carpenter perk now indicates how many steps until the next repair.
  • Active portals now have a distinct glow to let you know they are active.
  • Deleted barrier segments now have a thin line between them to make counting steps easier.


09/06/2026

Content changes:

  • New perk - EXTRA POWERUP: Lets you hold 3 powerups at the same time!
  • New perk - MAGICIAN: Dropping a ball creates a portal. With two portals, you can teleport!
  • New powerup - MAGNET: Warps you to the nearest powerup.
  • Removed powerup - SWITCH (flipped the totem's layers upside down; felt underwhelming)

Balancing changes:

  • The delete beam now spawns less frequently, and will only spawn a maximum of 10 times.
  • Warp powerups can now spawn slightly earlier.

Visual clarity improvements:

  • Deleted barriers now have their entire slice of the field darkened.
  • The missile layer is now more visually distinct from the standard beam layer.
  • The delete beam layer now draws more attention, to remind you of its presence.

Miscellaneous changes:

  • The in-game leaderboards are now refreshed whenever you submit a new highscore.


07/06/2026

  • PERKS!
  • Reverted the default amount of visible totem layers to 10 (experimental)

Around step 100 - and then again around step 300, 500, 700, ... - you'll be given a choice between two perks; each provides a permanent benefit for the rest of your run.
Like with totem layers and powerups, seeing a new perk appear will permanently unlock its information in the ℹ️-window.

Whether this is a cool new addition that makes each seed feel a bit more unique, or just feature creep? I'm not sure yet ;)



06/06/2026

  • Added an extra leaderboard that shows the highest scores of all time (in addition to the daily leaderboard)


05/06/2026

  • Deleted barriers now leave behind a more clear visual indicator


04/06/2026

  • Added the Delete Beam

After about 400 steps, all totem block and powerup variations will have shown up multiple times, and the game starts feeling a bit repetitive. To add some more strategic depth, the new Delete Beam - which starts spawning after 250 steps - *permanently* destroys a barrier every ~50 steps. This means you'll have to make lasting decisions about the layout of the field.

It is now actually difficult to reach 500, which speeds up the game nicely!
